- Allowed C: 300 deg/min.
- Effective F bottom: 94.248 mm/min.
- Result: limited by C-axis. PASS.

Case B: same F requested 650/730 mm/min, C max 10000 deg/min.
- Required C for bottom feed: 2069.014 deg/min.
- Allowed C: 10000 deg/min.
- Effective F bottom remains 650 mm/min.
- Result: not limited. PASS.

Case C: C max 300 deg/min, auto-limit disabled.
- Required C is above allowed C.
- Effective F remains requested F because limiter is off.
- UI reason: автоограничение выключено. PASS.
